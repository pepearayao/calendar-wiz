# calendar-wiz

A self-hosted system to aggregate my calendars (2 Gmail, 1 Proton, 1 Microsoft/Teams) into one
simple view, create/edit events across them, and share a booking link. Built in Kotlin as a
learning project. Possible iOS/Android app later.

Status: planning. The design is being charted with `/wayfinder`.

## Agent skills

### Issue tracker
